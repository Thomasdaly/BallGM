## C. ROOT-CAUSE CLUSTERING

1. **Rebounds and assists are apportioned after the game rather than recorded as events in the
   possession loop.** `FinishSide` turns the game's miss and make totals into rebound and assist counts
   with fixed shares and integer floors. This one cause drives:
   - the 1.4 leak (1.81 boards per team-game in 100% of games);
   - the 1.3 band miss (0.5683, below the 0.57 floor);
   - the near-zero game-level variance of assists (SD 0.0078);
   - the missing scorer–assister link (1.3).
   It also means an offensive rebound can't extend a possession or produce a second-chance point. The
   board is credited after the scoring has already been decided. That affects every rebounding and
   possession metric downstream, though none of them were scored because the audit halted.
2. **The box score charges events before their outcome is resolved.** FGA is incremented before the
   shooting-foul draw, so 8.08% of FGA (7.30 per team-game) are fouled misses the scoring convention
   wouldn't count as attempts. This is the second half of the 1.4 violation. Downstream (not scored,
   noted for the next run): box-score FG% is 0.461, against 0.502 with the phantom attempts excluded, so
   eFG% and TS% are biased low by the same mechanism.
3. **Simulated events that leave no box-score trace.** The 7.85 dead possessions per team-game record no
   turnover, team rebounds aren't a stat, and `UsagePercent` records an input weight rather than an
   observed outcome. None of these violate a conservation identity themselves (1.5 and 1.7 pass). They do
   mean any derived stat that reconstructs possessions or usage from the box score is wrong: the
   possession estimate is off by −8.99 per team-game, and usage is off by 2.59 pp per line.

## D. THE ONE THING

**Record every box-score figure at the possession event that produces it, inside the possession loop,
instead of apportioning totals afterwards in `FinishSide` and charging attempts before they resolve.**
Concretely:
- each live-ball miss resolves to exactly one recorded outcome: an offensive rebound that continues the
  possession, a defensive rebound, or a team rebound;
- an attempt is charged only once the foul draw has resolved it;
- an assist is decided per make and credited to a teammate of the scorer.

This is a mechanism change only. It specifies no parameter values. Expected effect on the three worst
metrics:
- **1.4 strict violation rate: 100% → 0%.** The leak goes from 1.81 to 0 because unclaimed boards become
  recorded team rebounds. Phantom misses go from 7.30 to 0 per team-game.
- **1.3 team AST/FGM: 0.5683 → about the stated 0.58,** inside the band, because no floor truncation is
  applied. The per-game SD rises from 0.0078 toward the ≈0.076 binomial level.
- **1.5 box-score possession bias: −8.99 → about −7.9.** The ORB term becomes meaningful once an offensive
  rebound genuinely extends a possession. Closing the rest would need the dead possession recorded as a
  turnover, which is the cluster-3 follow-up and outside this one change.

Risk: offensive rebounds that create second chances will raise points per possession, so `ortg`, pace and
the locked `MatchModelCalibrationTests` bands will need re-measuring with `/sim-regress` afterwards.

## Methodology

- **Sources and fixed seeds:**
  - **league:** 1,000 independent seasons of the shipped 6-team `FixtureLeagueDataSource`, fresh session
    per season, season seed = 1…1000. Per-game seeds are `SeasonSeed.ForGame(gameId)`. 247,614 games.
  - **chain:** 10 leagues × 20 chained seasons through `ConcludeSeason` (draft, AI turn, auto-resign; all
    200 seasons completed), seeds `7,000,000 + 100·L + s`. 49,594 games.
  - **pack:** 30 independent seasons of the user's local fictional 30-team sample pack (`local-packs/sample`,
    gitignored). Its schema-9 ruleset was copied to `scratch/audit/pack-sample/` with `schemaVersion` set to
    10, as the reader's own error message advises. Seeds 101…130. 39,477 games.
  - **controlled:** synthetic matchups with strength gaps −70…+70 (step 5; 1,500 each), rest days 1–3 and
    5–10-player rosters, plus 100,000 equal-strength games. Seeds `HashSeed(gap, i)` and
    `HashSeed(424242, i)`. 143,500 games.
- **Replica check:** the replica is the engine source with counters added and no change to RNG call order.
  `InstrumentedEngine` threw on any mismatch in score, stat line or injury count, and none occurred in
  480,185 games. The replica's own bookkeeping agreed with the box score on every team-game:
  `FGA−FGM = clean + fouled misses`, `boards = ORB + opp DRB`, and `internal misses = clean + trip-ending
  FT misses`.
- **Noise vs. bias:** the 1.4 rates are 100% with Wilson 95% lower bounds of at least 0.99995 in every
  source. The leak can't be zero for any game with a miss, because `0.02·M + frac(0.24·M) + frac(0.74·M)`
  is positive for every M > 0, so it is structural rather than sampling noise. The 1.3 league ratio sits
  more than 100 standard errors below the band floor.
- **No game code was edited** (`git status` clean). Analysis is pure-stdlib Python
  (`scratch/audit/analyze_b1.py`).
